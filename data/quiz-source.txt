Multiple Choice Questions

Topic 1: Access budget and financial plans for work team

Q1. Why is it important to access the team’s budget and financial plan?
a) To understand spending limits and priorities
b) To reduce the need for communication with management
c) To avoid following organisational procedures
d) To prepare contingency plans only when required
Answer: a) To understand spending limits and priorities

Q2. What is typically included in a financial plan?
a) Only staff salaries
b) Detailed travel allowances
c) Expected income, expenditure, and resource allocation
d) Marketing strategies only
Answer: c) Expected income, expenditure, and resource allocation

Q3. Which document provides detailed financial guidelines for a work team?
a) Employee handbook
b) Budget and financial plan
c) Training manual
d) Organisational chart
Answer: b) Budget and financial plan

Q4. Accessing a financial plan ensures that:
a) Work is aligned with financial objectives
b) Work can proceed without approvals
c) Spending can be ignored
d) Budgets can be hidden from the team
Answer: a) Work is aligned with financial objectives


Q5. Who is responsible for providing access to the budget plan?
a) Team members
b) Organisational management or finance department
c) Competitors
d) External consultants
Answer: b) Organisational management or finance department

Q6. Which of the following is not usually part of a team budget?
a) Salaries
b) Equipment
c) Utilities
d) Personal family expenses of staff
Answer: d) Personal family expenses of staff

Q7. What is the first step when a team leader receives a budget?
a) Allocate funds immediately
b) Review and understand budget objectives
c) Request more money
d) Ignore unnecessary details
Answer: b) Review and understand budget objectives

Q8. Access to budgets helps managers to:
a) Improve staff holidays
b) Allocate resources efficiently
c) Reduce work output
d) Avoid accountability
Answer: b) Allocate resources efficiently

Q9. Financial plans must always be:
a) Confidential to managers only
b) Flexible and aligned with organisational goals
c) Rigid and unchangeable
d) Based on individual preference
Answer: b) Flexible and aligned with organisational goals

Q10. Why is it necessary to check organisational guidelines when accessing budgets?
a) To ensure compliance and proper reporting
b) To create new guidelines
c) To limit team members’ access
d) To avoid financial objectives
Answer: a) To ensure compliance and proper reporting
