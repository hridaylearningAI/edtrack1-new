Multiple Choice Questions

Topic 1. Research models of critical and creative thinking

1.	Which model emphasizes six distinct thinking roles represented by metaphorical hats?
a) Bloom's Taxonomy
b) Six Thinking Hats
c) Socratic Method
d) SWOT Analysis
Answer: b) Six Thinking Hats

2.	Bloom's Taxonomy is primarily used for:
a) Encouraging creativity in the arts
b) Structuring levels of cognitive learning
c) Financial decision-making
d) Managing workplace safety
Answer: b) Structuring levels of cognitive learning

3.	The Socratic Method encourages critical thinking through:
a) Memorization
b) Questioning and dialogue
c) Group consensus
d) Visual aids
Answer: b) Questioning and dialogue
